Two more things a new hire has no way to know
  • Add — the ask-for line, shown on the panel: “Who schedules your outbound freight?” Not “the owner”, not “purchasing”. Seven calls ended at the wrong desk.
  • Add — a way to rule a company out. Tyler found by calling that Sord Landfill has no shipping team, and that Sawyer Fabrication does not take brokers. Both are real notes. The research step should ask the plain-English version first — do they move physical goods out of a building? — rather than “are they a shipper”, which means nothing on day one.
The one rule behind all of it

The panel is loud when the record is empty and quiet when it is full. Today it is equally grey either way, which is why a company nobody has researched looks exactly like one that is ready to call. Nothing here is written to the record without somebody pressing Yes.
