What the inventory shows
  • 25 distinct system event kinds all render identically — one grey line of summary text. The panel receives them pre-flattened to type: "activity", so it cannot tell a stage change from a deleted rate confirmation.
  • Two kinds — call and note_added — are duplicates of the real rows and are already excluded by the query. That is correct and should stay.
  • AI research briefs never reach this panel. All 6 are dropped by an is_ai filter upstream, so the six longest pieces of writing about these companies are invisible on the company profile. That is a separate question from this redesign — flagged, not changed.
